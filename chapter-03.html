Chapter 3: The Bed

Part 1

"My God, you're ugly."

Becky looked Duncan up and down, her sneer lingering on the way his stomach peeked out from beneath his stretched T-shirt.

HEY, BECKY. BE NICE.

She circled him slowly, assessing. "No ass. Like… at all." She frowned. "What do you even do?"

Duncan didn't respond.

He was too busy staring at Becky's D-cups bouncing beneath her sweater.

"Huh?"

Becky stopped. "Did you hear a single thing I just said?"

She was used to this. Men stared at her body for a living. It paid her rent.

"Uh—no," Duncan said honestly. "I'm Duncan." He slicked back his greasy hair and thrust out a hand.

The hand glistened with nervous sweat.

Becky looked at it.

Then she smacked it away with her staff.

"Ew. Gross. No. Keep your paws off me."

BECKY HAS JOINED THE PARTY.

"What was that?" Becky blinked. "Party? This doesn't look like a party."

Duncan, meanwhile, was staring at the new UI elements hovering over her head—basics like Becky's HP, MP, and any status effects appeared beside his own.

"Oh. Okay." He nodded to himself. "So. We're in an RPG."

Becky squinted. "A what?"

"Role-playing game," Duncan said, immediately slipping into full boring-nerd mode. "Like World of Warcraft or Dungeons & Dragons. Basically, we have stats, classes, quests, and—"

To her, Duncan sounded like a broken dishwasher—a rhythmic, grinding noise that had nothing to do with her life. She watched his mouth move as it started to hypnotize her to sleep. Her eyes glazed over completely.

Her head tilted slightly. One eyelid drooped.

"I need another bump," she muttered. "I'm getting sleepy."

NO, BECKY. NO MORE DRUGS. YOU TWO NEED TO REST.

A cabin blinked into existence beside them.

Duncan brightened. "Ladies first." He gestured toward the door.

Inside, the cabin was painfully simple. Wooden floor. One dresser. One queen-sized bed.

Becky didn't hesitate.

She climbed in, sprawled diagonally across the mattress, and yanked the blankets up to her chin.

Duncan froze.

"Hey! Leave some room for me!"

Becky rolled onto her side. "You sleep on the floor."

ACHIEVEMENT: ALPHA MENALITY. You don't need no man. (And he don't need no bed space, apparently.)

Duncan frowned. He pouted. He threw a mini temper tantrum. "Can we get another bed in here AI?"

NO.

"I can't sleep on the floor. She's already got the bed." Duncan stammered.

NO.

"Becky, scootch over." Becky was already sleeping by this time. A bit of drool flowing out of the corner of her mouth.

"For fuck's sake."

He curled up at the foot of the bed like a chastised dog, tugging a sad corner of the blanket over himself.

Duncan stared at the ceiling.

The party had begun.

Part 2

"Are you fucking kidding me?"

Duncan muttered under his breath as Becky's snoring sawed through the cabin like a chainsaw with asthma. His hangover was crashing hard now, the Jäger warmth long gone, leaving only a throbbing headache and a floor that felt like cold concrete wrapped in splinters.

"AI, seriously, bro. Can I get something to sleep on? Anything? A mat? A pile of hay? I'm begging."

NO. STOP ASKING. SLEEP IN THE BED.

The reply was curt, almost bored.

Duncan threw off the pathetic corner of blanket he'd managed to steal and stood up.

He looked down at Becky.

Her hair was a rat's nest, mascara had raccooned around her eyes, and the pillow—Jesus Christ, the pillow—was soaked in drool. A lake of it.

Then he heard it.

Pffft.

The smell hit half a second later: sulfur, skunk, and something that had clearly died inside her earlier.

"Jesus Christ, Becky."

"Wha…?" She rolled over, half-asleep.

"You just crop-dusted the entire cabin," Duncan said, pinching his nose shut. "And you snore like a goddamn freight train."

"Oh." Becky yanked the blanket over her face and resumed snoring.

Duncan's patience snapped like a cheap guitar string.

He ripped the blanket off her in one yank.

Becky's eyes flew open. She sat up, clutching at the empty air.

"Hey! I'm cold! Gimme that back!"

"Did you hear a single word I said?" Duncan snapped, sounding exactly like a raid leader scolding a DPS who stood in fire.

"Yes," Becky shot back, suddenly defensive. "Guys pay me good money to record me doing exactly that stuff. What's your problem?"

"I'm not one of your simps, Becky. I'm Duncan. I don't give a flying fuck how hot you think you are, or actually are. I'm sleeping in this bed."

"Eww. No." She immediately sprawled out starfish-style, claiming every inch her 110-pound frame could possibly reach.

Duncan had hit his limit.

He didn't ask again.

He just dropped straight down onto the mattress beside her—hard enough that the bedframe creaked in protest. Her left arm ended up pinned beneath his bulk.

Becky shrieked like she'd been set on fire.

ACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED: ALPHA MENALITY. That's it, take what your entire 280lb frame is entitled to. Fuck that bitch. Or not—clearly not.

"Get it OFF me! Eww, eww, eww!"

"Fuck you, Becky." Duncan yanked the blanket over himself, rolled toward the edge, and finally let her arm free.

"I swear to God," she hissed, "if you touch me—"

"Not interested, fart machine."

Becky glared at the back of his head for three full seconds, then snatched her half of the blanket with a huff.

They lay there in angry silence, two strangers sharing a mattress and a mutual loathing.

Eventually, exhaustion won.

They both passed out—shoulders almost touching, blankets drawn like battle lines down the middle of the bed.

Part 3

RISE AND SHINE.

The AI didn't bother easing them into consciousness. The roof of the cabin simply vanished, peeling away and dumping raw sunlight straight onto Duncan and Becky.

"Oh—holy fuck—no."

Duncan's eyes cracked open. His skull throbbed like it was being used as a drum in a bad metal band. The sunlight felt less like light and more like an act of violence.

Becky, meanwhile, remained unconscious.

She was deep in the post-bender abyss—crashing hard after stimulants, booze, and spite.

That's when a bird flew overhead.

It hovered for half a second.

Then, without ceremony or mercy, it unloaded.

The payload landed directly in Becky's hair and bit splattering on her shirt.

Duncan, feeling like death reheated in a bargain-bin air fryer, laughed anyway. A weak, wheezing laugh—but sincere.

Becky stirred.

She felt it before she understood it. Warm. Wet. Sticky.

Her eyes snapped open.

"What the fuck, pervert!" She screamed and immediately kicked Duncan in the ribs. "Did you just splooge in my hair? My hair? Of all places?"

"Dude—no!" Duncan gasped, curling away. "It was a bird!"

Becky froze.

"…A bird? A fucking bird?" She reached up, felt the mess, brought her fingers in front of her face. White, warm, viscous. "What kind of bird does drive-by splooging?"

"Dude, I swear to God, I'm not—"

Then Becky—without thinking—brought her finger to her mouth and tasted it.

She immediately gagged and spat violently.

Duncan's eyes went wide. Half-grinning at her sheer idiocy, half-nauseous from watching her eat bird shit.

"Ok, fine," Becky conceded, wiping her tongue on the blanket. "Tastes bitter, not salty."

ACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED: WTF? - Seriously Becky, that's how you determine if it was actually bird shit? It was thick, whitish-yellow… oh, nm, I get it now.

ANWAY, YOU TWO LOVE BIRDS READY TO START YOUR QUEST?

"Not gonna lie, AI," Duncan groaned, muscles cramping from withdrawal, hands starting to shake. "I don't think I can move."

POOF.

A half-pint of Five O'Clock vodka appeared beside him.

"Seriously? This shit." Duncan griped, popping the cap and taking a swig. The burn was pure rubbing alcohol—harsh, chemical, like licking a battery—but it steadied the shakes. "Can I get a chaser?"

POOF.

A can of V8 juice materialized next to it.

"Thanks, bro!" Duncan cracked it open, took a careful sip—just enough to mask the vodka's bite.

"Hey, what about me?" Becky whined and pouted, looking adorable, or at least trying to.

POOF.

A fifth of Malibu coconut rum appeared beside her, pristine and beautiful, inviting.

Becky cracked it open without hesitation, took a long, defiant swig, and sighed like she'd just been handed salvation.

"What the fuck, AI?" Duncan sputtered, still clutching his shitty vodka. "You give her Malibu? Seriously?"

AT LEAST YOU GOT V8.

Becky lowered the bottle, lips shiny with rum. "Shut it, bonehead. Where's my coke?"

I TOLD YOU. NO MORE DRUGS. BOTH OF YOU, YOU GET WHAT YOU GET AND DON'T THROW A FIT.

"Lame."

She flopped back onto the pillow—and took another pull.

NOW ON WITH IT.

The quest notification appeared again. It hovered in the air telling them to find Vern.

Then, without notice, the cabin and bed blinked out of existence, leaving the two with their asses on the grass.

END OF CHAPTER.
