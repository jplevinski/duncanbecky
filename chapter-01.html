Chapter 1: Introductions

Part 1: Duncan

"Motherfucking—bitch—cock—Jesus Christ."

Duncan clutched his skull as if it were trying to escape him. Two facts surfaced through the nausea:

One: the erection was gone.
Two: so was his dignity.

He leaned forward and vomited.

Swirling user interfaces and hangovers did not mix.

He pinched himself hard. Nothing changed. No basement. No gaming chair. No piss bottles. Just open sky and grass that looked suspiciously high-resolution.

CHARACTER SELECTION COMMENCES, the AI blared.

"Screw you," Duncan groaned. "Stop shouting. I have a headache."

CHARACTER SELECTION COMMENCES. PLEASE CHOOSE A RACE AND CLASS.

"Argh—what the hell is this?" Duncan squinted around. The open field stretched on forever, empty except for floating menus and glowing panels. It looked exactly like the start of an MMORPG.

"Oh, fuck me."

He pressed his temples, dry heaved twice, then burped.

"Well," he said, straightening up, "this is kinda cool."

Duncan didn't question it. Questioning things was sober behavior.

"What are my choices, hum de dum de dum…" He flicked through menus with the bored efficiency of a veteran gamer, scanning races and classes between waves of nausea. He knew all this. Stats. Synergies. Builds.

"Wish I had a damned shot."

Duncan looked around the field.

Then he smiled.

"Oh shit. Motherfuckers left me a treat."

A pint of Jägermeister sat in the grass like an offering from a benevolent god. Duncan snatched it up and took a long pull. Warmth bloomed through his chest. The headache eased. The world made sense again.

"Mmm… better."

Duncan—now firmly in the zone—returned to character creation.

"What the fuck, no gender options? I wanted to be a hot female Night Elf." He frowned. "I wanted to really experience womanhood. Not just… manage my manhood."

"Choices. Choices. Choices." He skimmed the races. Human. Elf. Dwarf. Gnome. Orc. Troll. "Same shit."

SO YOU WOULD LIKE SOMETHING NEW. HOW ABOUT A MURDER-HOBO BOX? MAYBE A CLASSLESS PROTO-SPECIES? BORING SHIT ONLY HERE. NOW PICK.

"Fuck," Duncan muttered.

The avatars hovered nearby—perfect bodies, six-pack abs, thick legs, heroic jawlines. Every one of them was aggressively well-endowed.

Duncan nodded thoughtfully.

"Hey, AI. Which race has the biggest shlongs?"

The avatars all rolled their eyes. At once. With commitment.

WOULDN'T YOU LIKE TO KNOW. PICK A DAMN RACE ALREADY.

"I pick the race with the biggest penis."

WELL. AREN'T YOU ANATOMICALLY PRECISE. HUMAN IT IS.

POOF.

Nothing changed.

Duncan stared down at himself.

"…What the fuck? Nothing changed."

He stomped the grass and crossed his arms, pouting like a prepubescent porky kid.

IT'S NOT THE SIZE OF THE MEMBER. IT'S THE WAY THE HIPS MOVE. WITHIN REASON.

"You know," Duncan said bitterly, "if I'm getting a do-over, I'd like to be a little better equipped. Fucker. I haven't gotten laid in like… six years."

He grabbed his junk, flipped off the sky, and moved on.

"Alright. Class selection. No tricks this time, right?"

OH, SURE. NONE AT ALL.

"Prick."

Duncan scanned the list. "Warrior. Warlock. Paladin. Rogue. Hunter…" He scratched himself absently. "These are just WoW classes. Am I dreaming?"

YOU WISH. HURRY UP, NOOB.

"Okay, okay. I'm gonna be like Boxxy. Warlock!"

WARRIOR IT IS.

"No, I said Warlock." Duncan punctuated the word this time.

WARRIOR IT IS.

"Son of a bitch."

Duncan glanced around, instinctively searching for his mom. This game was cheating. First human. Now a warrior. Absolute bullshit.

"Look at me," he snapped at the sky. "I'm fat. Out of shape. Horny without release. And drunk. You think warrior works?"

WARRIOR. IT. IS.

POOF.

Duncan stared at the numbers.

Then he laughed—a short, bitter bark that ended in a hiccup.

"Of course."

The pint of Jäger was still in his hand. He raised it toward the empty sky.

"Nice one, fucker."

And took a huge swig.

Part 2: Becky

"What an asshole."

Becky wiped at her mascara-streaked eyes with the heel of her hand. "Fuck Brad. Fuck him. I can do better."

CHARACTER SELECTION COMMENCES.

"What… is this?" Becky squinted.

In the middle of the sun-drenched field sat a folding table straight out of a brunch-themed intervention. Mimosas. Bloody Marys. Celery stalks like judgmental green fingers.

Becky waddled over and downed two drinks without stopping to breathe.

"Whew. Okay. I needed that. I'm fine now."

She glanced back at the floating menus. "Awww… look at the cute little people." Becky leaned closer, squinting at the gnomes. "What is this supposed to be?"

HAVEN'T YOU EVER PLAYED AN RPG IN YOUR LIFE?

She frowned at the screen. "RPG? What's that?"

"What is this?" Becky said, mostly to herself. "I must've taken shrooms. I didn't think I did, but honestly, who knows anymore."

Her words came out slower than she intended.

DAMMIT, BECKY. GET A HOLD OF YOUR LIFE.

"Oh—geez—you—you…" Becky blinked. "What was I saying?"

She bent forward and puked.

The vomit splashed against the translucent UI, droplets sliding down the holographic gold lettering of the Gnome race description.

DISGUSTING. ABSOLUTELY RECOIL-INDUCING.

"It's… it's a de—de—detoxxx," Becky slurred, wiping her mouth with the back of a hand that still sported a hundred-dollar manicure. "Brad liked detoxes. Brad also liked Emily. Ugh."

LOOK, I CAN'T HAVE YOU DYING IN THE FIRST FIVE MINUTES. IT REFLECTS POORLY ON MY METRICS. SINCE YOU'RE CLEARLY COMMITTED TO THE 'HOT MESS' AESTHETIC, TAKE THIS AND SHUT UP.

[PITY STAT GRANTED!]
+1 CHARISMA
Note: You look like a dumpster fire, but somehow people still want to roast marshmallows over you. Congratulations, Hotty.

"Ooooh, a gift?" Becky giggled, swaying slightly. "Is it a filter? Because this lighting is not doing me favors. Where are the ring lights?"

From somewhere across the field, a distant voice screamed, "What the fuck? That's not fair! She got a stat boost for just existing?"

"Pretty privilege," Becky muttered. "Haters gonna—"

She barfed again.

THERE ARE NO RING LIGHTS, BECKY. THERE IS ONLY THE COLD, HARD REALITY OF YOUR NEW BUILD. AND SINCE YOU CLEARLY CRAVE ATTENTION, WE'RE GOING TO LEAN INTO THAT.

"Oh, sorry." Becky waved vaguely. "Just need a little coke. You got any?"

OMFG. FINE.

POOF.

A small table appeared. A neat line of coke waited patiently.

Becky didn't hesitate.

BETTER, BECKY?

"So much better," she sighed. "Thanks, babe."

She scrolled through the races, making faces. "Eww. Uggh. Why are they all like that?"

She stopped.

"Huh. Human works."

CONGRATULATIONS. YOU PICKED HUMAN. NO NEED TO CHANGE, AM I RIGHT?

"Hey, seriously?" Duncan's voice echoed again. "She gets to pick her race?"

"Who's that?" Becky asked, side-eyeing the rotund nerd hovering in the background.

HE'S YOUR NEW BOYFRIEND.

"Oh, hell no." Becky gagged as she looked at Duncan.

JESUS, GIRL. HOW MUCH DID YOU DRINK?

"Not enough," she snapped. "I need more."

NOT UNTIL YOU PICK A CLASS.

Becky crossed her arms and pouted. "Fine. What am I?"

OMG, BECKY. LOOK AT YOUR CLASS. YOU'RE A PRIEST.

POOF!

Becky, Level 1 Priest

HAPPY, DUNCAN? I PICKED HERS TOO.

"Yes!" Duncan snorted. "Thank you, AI!"

Becky's perfectly plucked eyebrows narrowed. "Are you serious? The last religious thing I did involved Brad, a crucifix, and some very poor decision-making. And now you want me to be a priest?"

JESUS, BECKY.

END OF CHAPTER.
