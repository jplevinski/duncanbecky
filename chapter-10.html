Chapter 10: Keep Duncan Soberish

Part 1

The duo walked. Duncan stayed tanked, but he sobered up fast—unnaturally fast. He felt the buzz evaporate from his blood.

IT'S BECAUSE I INCREASED HIS ALCOHOL METABOLISM.

The AI seethed with hostility, for whatever reason. The breathalyzer was destroyed, and the only booze left in Duncan's inventory was some Mystery Spud Juice.

"I'm gonna do it. Fuck it." Duncan uncorked the Mystery Spud Juice and chugged.

It hit him like an ogre's wang at a monster orgy.

Wham! Duncan folded like a lawn chair and face-planted into the soil, dead to the world.

"The fuck, Duncan?" Becky stopped and neared his snoring, face-down body. "Get up—we've got to complete the quest."

Duncan didn't stir.

Becky kicked him lightly in the side. Nothing. She drew back her foot and kicked harder.

-10 HP

"Duncan!" Becky screamed into his ear. She let out a guttural, furious "Ugh!" She grabbed his arm and pushed forward as best she could. The ethereal chain pulled taut. She dug her sneakers into the earth and threw her body weight forward, trying to drag 280 pounds of deadweight flesh. She didn't budge him an inch.

"Maybe I should get a running start," Becky muttered.

She stepped backward, clambering over Duncan's body for maximum slack, then sprinted forward with everything she had.

As anyone with a basic grasp of physics could predict, the bracelet locked at the end of the slack. The chain yanked her with the force of an ogre's climax. Becky's feet flew out from under her. She whiplashed backward and landed brutally hard on her upper back.

The air exploded from her lungs. She gagged on her own tongue, staring up as her HP bar flashed.

"Damn it, AI!" she wheezed. "At least he's drunk—Vader stays safe, for now."

Becky scanned the area, hunting for anything to help move Duncan's monolithic ass.

Then she spotted it in the distance. Nothing good, of course: a duo of goblins. She inspected them.

Goblin Distiller: the place where Dragon's Piss gets made.

And, naturally, they headed straight for her and Duncan.

"Jesus H. mother-fucker." Becky yanked on the chain to drag Duncan. Maybe adrenaline surged, maybe her badassery kicked in, but that fat fuck budged an inch. Then another, and another. Wouldn't you know it—Becky won this tug-of-war.

Or maybe not. She moved too slow. The goblins closed in.

"What'cha doin', sweetheart?" a goblin leered, as another whistled at her. "You got a nice cask on ya. Wanna come back to the stills and let us ferment you?" the goblin added, as one of his face pustules leaked.

Becky froze. She pondered for a second: maybe a show would pacify them. So she slipped into full OnlyFans mode.

"Oh, baby, treat me well and maybe I will." Becky slid her robe down to her shoulders, exposing her cleavage, then bent over slightly to let the girls peek more.

"I'll bottle you up and drink you down," one goblin snickered. "I'm gonna shove my spigot right in your eye socket," another growled.

"My, what?" That was new—she'd never had anyone want that. Then it dawned on her: that would cost at least one eye. Fuck, she thought. She'd have to fight these sons of bitches alone.

Part 2

The brief surge of adrenaline that had helped Becky drag Duncan vanished. Her arms shook. Her head pounded, and the stale taste of bottom-shelf vodka coated her tongue.

She spat into the grass and shoved a sweaty clump of dark hair out of her eyes.

"Okay, you perverted little fuckers," she rasped, swaying into something that almost resembled a fighting stance. "Which one of you wants your ass kicked first?"

The two goblin distillers stood several yards away, grinning at her. They smelled like rotten cabbage, sour mash, and unwashed ass. The one with the leaking pustule licked his cracked lips.

"Oh, we got a prime vintage here," he said, looking her up and down. "Little bruised. Little sweaty. Just how I like 'em."

His companion whistled.

SYSTEM NOTIFICATION: NEW ENCOUNTER TRIGGERED
ENEMIES: TWO GOBLIN DISTILLERS
DEBUFF ACTIVE: [HAIR OF THE DOG]
ACCURACY REDUCED BY 40%
MAGIC COSTS INCREASED BY 15%
CAUSE: SEVERE ALCOHOL WITHDRAWAL AND POOR LIFE CHOICES

Becky clenched her trembling hands.

"Smite!"

A burst of holy light shot toward the pustule-faced goblin and struck him square in the nose.

There was a wet crunch.

-96 HP

Green blood sprayed across his mouth. He dropped to his knees, clutching what remained of his snout.

"My nose!" he shrieked. "How am I supposed to sniff sweaty hotties now?"

"Try using your personality."

The second goblin barely glanced at his wounded friend. His attention stayed fixed on Becky.

"I like a squirmy batch," he said, drawing a rusted shiv. "Let me pop your cork and distill your insides."

Becky blinked at him.

For one brief second, her hangover was replaced by pure disgust.

Then her eyes lowered toward his crotch.

"I warned you."

The goblin grinned. "About what?"

"Smite."

Holy light flashed between them.

The spell struck the goblin directly in his walnut-shaped sack. Divine energy sliced through skin and connective tissue with surgical precision.

Both testicles detached.

They hit the grass with two wet, heavy thuds.

The goblin stared down.

His mouth opened, but no sound came out.

Becky waited.

The scream finally arrived three seconds later.

It came out several octaves higher than his speaking voice.

"You fucking cunt!"

Blinded by pain, the goblin charged her, wildly swinging the shiv at her chest.

"Holy Shield!"

A barrier of yellow light flickered into existence between them. The rusty blade struck the shield and bounced away with a metallic clang.

The barrier absorbed the force of the attack and converted it into healing energy.

[75 DAMAGE ABSORBED.]
[75 HP RESTORED.]

Warmth spread through Becky's body. It dulled the pounding in her skull, but the shaking in her hands remained.

The goblin stabbed again.

His blade scraped across the fading barrier.

"You cut off my balls!"

"You were going to put your manhood in my eye socket."

"That's different!"

"How?"

"I need my balls!"

Becky's patience snapped.

"Smite."

A column of holy light slammed into the goblin's chest and knocked him onto his back.

He hit the ground hard enough to bounce.

Becky raised one hand.

"Smite."

Another blast struck his shoulder, crushing bone beneath the skin.

"Wait!" he screamed.

"Smite."

The next spell tore through his ribs.

The goblin clawed at the dirt, trying to crawl away.

Becky followed him with her hand.

"Smite."

Holy light crashed down again.

"Smite."

Green blood splashed across the grass.

"SMITE!"

The final blast reduced the goblin to a steaming mound of shattered bone, torn flesh, and bubbling green sludge.

[MANA: 0/240.]
[ENEMY DEFEATED: GOBLIN DISTILLER.]

Becky stood over the remains, panting.

"Should've kept your mouth shut."

SYSTEM AI: THAT WAS EXCESSIVE.

"He threatened my eye."

SYSTEM AI: YOU CAST SMITE SIX TIMES AFTER HE LOST THE ABILITY TO REPRODUCE.

"He was still holding a knife."

SYSTEM AI: MOST OF HIM WAS NO LONGER HOLDING ANYTHING.

Becky turned toward the other goblin.

He was gone.

The pustule-faced distiller had abandoned his injured nose and sprinted halfway across the clearing. His short legs pumped furiously as he fled toward the trees.

"Hey!" Becky shouted. "Get back here!"

The goblin looked over his shoulder.

His gaze dropped toward his dead companion's testicles lying in the grass.

He immediately ran faster.

[ENEMY FLED.]
[0 EXP AWARDED.]
[COWARDICE PENALTY APPLIED.]
[NEW TITLE UNLOCKED: THE NUTCRACKER SUITE]
Rarity: Legendary
Effect: Male enemies who witness you destroying another creature's reproductive organs suffer a 15% chance to flee.

SYSTEM AI: I DO NOT POSSESS PHYSICAL ANATOMY. HOWEVER, CALCULATING THE TRAJECTORY OF THOSE TESTICLES CAUSED SEVERAL OF MY PROCESSES TO SHUT DOWN IN SELF-DEFENSE.

"Good," Becky muttered. "Maybe you'll stop talking."

She bent over, placed her hands on her knees, and tried not to vomit.

Behind her, Duncan stirred.

[STATUS EFFECT: STILL DRUNK.]
[INTELLIGENCE: -50.]
[CONFUSION: +100.]

"Fuuuuuck…"

Duncan peeled his face away from the ground like a damp piece of ham. Dirt and blades of grass clung to his cheek.

He blinked at the scene before him.

The clearing looked like a fantasy-themed meat grinder had exploded. Green blood soaked the grass. Chunks of goblin flesh steamed in the sunlight. Faint sparks of holy energy drifted above the remains.

Duncan squinted at the smoking, ball-less stain.

"What the hell happened?"

Becky straightened and wiped sweat from her forehead.

"You passed out."

"I gathered that."

"I had to fight two goblins alone."

"Did you win?"

"One ran away."

Duncan nodded slowly, as though processing a complicated philosophical argument.

"What happened to the other one?"

Becky looked toward the pulpy remains.

Then she looked at the two severed testicles sitting several feet away in the grass.

Part 3

"Get up, you fat drunk," Becky snapped, violently tugging at the ethereal chain linking their wrists. "I can't go anywhere with you completely out of it. We are chained together, remember? Also, I just committed a war crime on a goblin's crotch and I need a drink."

Duncan groaned, peeling his face off the grass. His [Satiety Meter] was flashing an aggressive, starving red, completely overriding his hangover. "Holy fuck, I'm hungry."

And that's when his crusty eyes spotted them. Sitting right there in the dirt, perfectly separated from their previous owner: two plump, greenish-grey, delicious-looking meatballs. They were practically glistening in the Mellonwood sun.

[ITEM DISCOVERED: Freshly Detached Goblin Oysters]
Rarity: Uncomfortably Common
Nutritional Value: Negative 50 Dignity
Description: Not meatballs.

Duncan didn't read the prompt. He immediately started commando-crawling toward his prize, his 280-pound Wrecker frame wiggling through the brush like a ravenous, heavily intoxicated walrus.

"Duncan, what are you doing?" Becky asked, noticing his pathetic trajectory.

"Those look so tasty," Duncan slurred, his crawl accelerating as his [Gluttony] stat hijacked his basic motor functions.

Becky looked from him, to the bubbling puddle of holy goblin soup, and back to the severed testicles. "No, no, no. That's not food."

"What? What are you talking about?" Duncan pushed himself off the ground, drunkenly swaying toward his ground-snack. "It's lil' meatballs. They left us an appetizer."

Becky paused. Her mind flashed back to the bird shit incident—how she had willingly scooped that creamy, warm sludge into her own mouth just to test it. The earthy, bitter flavor hitting her lips. Was she really in a position to judge?

"Duncan, those are literally—"

She didn't even finish her sentence. Duncan had already arrived at his destination. He scooped one up with the reverence of a man finding an extra chicken nugget in his drive-thru bag.

"Still warm," he whispered affectionately, right before shoving half of the golf-ball-sized mass into his mouth.

The Goblin Oyster—for lack of a more horrifying term—detonated between his molars. It flooded his palate with a gush of warm, salty, inexplicably slimy fluid.

SYSTEM AI: I have monitored trillions of data points across the multiverse, witnessed the birth and death of stars, and calculated the absolute limits of human stupidity. And yet, watching you willingly eat a raw goblin testicle off the dirt is, without question, the most revolting thing I have ever had the misfortune to process. You owe my servers an apology.

Duncan chewed thoughtfully, completely ignoring the celestial entity judging his dietary habits.

"Mm…" Duncan smacked his lips, swallowing the gelatinous horror with a satisfied burp. "Salty. Kinda tastes like a meaty Canterbury Cream egg."
