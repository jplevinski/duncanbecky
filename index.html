The Story

Duncan owns a Munchkin cat, lives in his mother's basement, and drinks too much Jägermeister. Becky is an influencer funding her existence through the generosity of men, and she drinks too much Malibu. After a legendary blackout, they both wake up in a sun-drenched, high-resolution field with a floating UI and a deeply hostile AI.

Forced to team up as a Level 1 Wrecker and a Level 1 Shadow Caster, this is the story of two people who can't even manage their own lives, let alone a fantasy world. Between fending off perverted goblins, managing their crippling hangovers, and trying not to kill each other, they have one ultimate quest: keep Duncan's blood-alcohol level high enough to save his cat, Meowth Vader, from permanent deletion.

Hostile LitRPG Mechanics

No helpful tutorials here. Level up, pick a class, and try to understand the system before the AI hits you with a devastating debuff for asking stupid questions.

Unapologetic Anti-Heroes

They aren't noble. They aren't min-maxing geniuses. They are just trying to find their next drink and maybe some decent loot along the way.

A Ticking Clock

The "Keep Duncan Sober(ish)" quest mechanic guarantees the stakes stay high. Metabolize too fast? The cat gets it. Spike too high? Blackout and die.
