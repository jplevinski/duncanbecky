Chapter 9: Life Berries

She looked smaller now—not physically, but emotionally. Less sharp. Less armored.

He was someone who cared.

Even if what he cared about was a fat, smushy-faced cat.

"Hey—stop!"

"Duncan!" Becky scrambled, panic flashing across her face as she realized just how strong he was. "Stop—please!"

He stopped.

Breathing hard.

He turned.

"I used to play football. I had cheerleaders. I had scouts for Division One." His jaw clenched. "Then in my junior year some asshole cheap-shotted me and snapped my femur."

Silence.

Becky stared.

This guy—this fat, angry, drunken geek—had once been that guy.

"Oh," she whispered.

"Depression and pills fucked everything up," Duncan said quietly. "In here, though—my bones are healed."

Becky didn't answer right away.

She just stared at him.

Her mental picture of Duncan—slob, pervert, loser—collapsed all at once, like bad scaffolding. This wasn't some basement-dwelling creep dragging her through a quest. This was a man who had been something. An athlete. Confident. Wanted. The kind of guy girls used to orbit, hoping for a glance, a smile—anything.

And somehow, that version of him still existed.

Right here.

"Fuck," Becky thought.
