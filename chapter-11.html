Chapter 11: Grand-Daddy's Kombucha

Part 1

"You think Vern'll like the other one?" Duncan slurred, cradling the remaining green-grey goblin oyster in his palms like a delicate, fleshy treasure. His Wrecker-class brain was currently operating on zero cylinders. "I mean, I'll eat it, but maybe he'd like a taste? You wanna try?" He thrust the severed gonad right up to Becky's nose.

"Ew, no, get it away from me!" Becky shrieked, batting his hand away as her own fingers trembled from her Malibu detox. "We need to check your BAC, you absolute psycho." She tapped her temple, pulling up her Party Inspection HUD since the fat idiot had smashed the physical breathalyzer to pieces earlier.

A flashing red notification popped up over his head:

STATUS: CATASTROPHICALLY WASTED. BAC: 0.351

"Duncan, are you blacked out?" she gasped.

"No, I remember everything. Oh, look, I'm holding a meatball!" Duncan exclaimed, marveling at the severed appendage as if it had miraculously spawned from his inventory. "Where'd this come from?"

Without hesitation, he popped it into his mouth. "Damn, tastes a bit like it was seared on a Blackstone griddle with butter, garlic, and fresh rosemary."

SYSTEM AI: I GUESS VERN WON'T GET TO "ENJOY" THAT SNACK.

Becky gagged. "Oh my fucking God," she spat, dry heaves wracking her frame as she watched the walrus swallow the second one. "You're fucking disgusting!"

"Huh? Shut the fuck up, you only-fuck'n-fans, fucking, ho-bag, dil-bert stroke," Duncan attempted to snap, but the insult tapered off into a confused, slurred mush as his [Gluttony] buff wore off.

"At least you won't remember this, but it'll be etched into my mind forever," Becky groaned, her HP flashing slightly from the sheer psychological trauma. She suggested they just rest, dropping her staff in the dirt.

They both collapsed onto the sun-drenched earth. Becky spent the next twenty minutes swatting glowing, low-res gnats off her legs, bitterly complaining that the hard ground felt absolutely nothing like the comfort of a queen-sized air mattress layered with a quilt while car camping. Eventually, the sheer weight of exhaustion—and Duncan's gargantuan alcohol intake—pulled them under, and they fell asleep.

What seemed like mere seconds later, the server apparently decided they'd suffered enough peace. They awoke groggily to find the sun-drenched field gone. They were huddled together near a roaring campfire that felt like it belonged in some random wannabe cowboy's backyard, entirely surrounded by a hostile, chattering camp of low-level mobs.

Hovering above the creatures were their floating UI nametags: [Level 4: Pimp Imp], [Level 4: Simp Imp], and, for some absolutely inexplicable reason, a group of short-statured human women simply labeled [Level 5: Dwarve Prostitute].

"Our guests, our guests have woken, oh yes, oh yes." The Pimp Imp hopped around the captive duo like a meth-addled leprechaun. His voice possessed the distinct, raspy charm of an eighty-year-old who had just inhaled a carton of Marlboro Reds.

Duncan's crusty eyes snapped open. "I need alcohol!" he bellowed, his Wrecker frame thrashing against the coarse ropes. "I feel like I'm sobering up!"

A terrifying hallucination of his beloved Munchkin cat, Meowth Vader, flashed across his vision.

QUEST WARNING: KEEP DUNCAN SOBER(ISH)
OH NO, BAC IS DROPP'N, CAT'S HEAD ABOUT TO BE POPP'N?

"Let me fucking go!" Duncan roared. He strained against his bonds. The friction burned with the chafing intensity of a dry-hump gone horribly wrong. Then, the ropes started cutting into his skin, drawing blood like... well, let's just say it looked like a visit from Aunt Flo and leave it at that. We aren't earning our Red Wings in this particular adventure.

Channeling every ounce of grit his Level 5 Wrecker stats could muster, Duncan gave it his all.

He didn't break the ropes.

Instead, he let out a massive, steaming, unapologetically wet fart.

STATUS AILMENT AFFLICTED: THE LIQUOR SHITS
Description: The consequences of eating raw Goblin Oysters off the dirt have arrived. -10 to Dignity. Area of Effect: Point-blank.

Because they were literally bound together by the ethereal Butt Buddies chain, Becky took the brunt of the splash damage.

"You absolute fuck!" Becky shrieked, her Shadow Caster robes now officially ruined.

"I have the liquor shits! But I need more booze, or Meowth Vader's gonna…" Duncan couldn't finish the sentence. Pure, unadulterated rage flooded his system. He was mad. Big mad. She-used-me-for-a-free-dinner-on-Tinder mad. He glared at the sky and screamed at the system. "If you let my cat die, I will hunt you down until every last tentacle of your wiring is eradicated into the abyss!"

SYSTEM AI: I DON'T HAVE WIRES, YOU MORON. I'M HOSTED ON A CLOUD SERVER. BUT PLEASE, KEEP THREATENING THE ENTITY THAT CONTROLS YOUR SPHINCTER.

"Relax," a small, gravelly voice interrupted. "We've got some Kombucha."

"Kombucha? Really?" Duncan squinted at the murky liquid. "That looks exactly like dirty tea!"

"No, no. It's fermented. Have a taste." The Pimp Imp, whose floating nametag proudly declared him as [Level 4: Grand-Daddy-Flashes-A-Lot], shoved a freshly brewed Imp Kombucha toward Duncan's face.

Despite being hogtied by perverted goblins, Duncan made the highly questionable decision to chug it anyway. Honestly, after raw testicles, it wasn't the worst thing he'd had in his mouth today.

CONSUMABLE USED: IMP KOMBUCHA
Warning: BAC has spiked dangerously high. Cease drinking immediately or face critical organ failure.

"Breathalyze me!" Duncan demanded.

For reasons that defied all logic, Grand-Daddy-Flashes-A-Lot reached into his filthy waistband and produced a breathalyzer.

"Blow," the Imp whispered.

Duncan started to blow. Grand-Daddy started to moan. "Keep blowin'... yeah, that's it."

Duncan obliged only because his UI hadn't registered a reading yet. But just as the breathalyzer was about to hit its climax, Grand-Daddy whipped open his loincloth, flashing Duncan and Becky and revealing… well. You know. Let's just say the LitRPG tag doesn't cover this level of psychological trauma.

"What the—" Duncan gasped, just as the digital readout finally flashed across his vision.

CURRENT BAC: 4.2
STATUS: HOW ARE YOU NOT DEAD?

Part 2

"Holy Jesus, can I get a hit of that Kombucha?" Becky whined as her head pounded from withdrawals.

She actively wrestled her gaze away from Grand-daddy's exposed... situation. Years of surviving the worst the United States dating pool had to offer had taught her one universal law: if you make eye contact with the unsolicited meat-stick, then they immediately expect something. Still, the appendage was so bizarrely misshapen and aggressively colored that it piqued her [Morbid Curiosity] stat. She kind of wanted to peek. Just for science. And it looked a little like a Catajizz'm Bad Dragon dildo.

"Oh, you don't need a heavy pour like this big 'ole boy," Grand-daddy cackled, generously passing her a shot-sized smudge of his Imp Kombucha.

Becky didn't hesitate. She threw it back like a desperate bachelorette at last call.

STATUS EFFECT APPLIED: INSTA-TRASHED
BAC boosted by 4.0. Intelligence decreased to 0. Dignity permanently deleted.

SYSTEM AI: DAMNIT BECKY, AND HERE I THOUGHT YOU HAD BEEN THROUGH ENOUGH BAD SITUATIONS IN YOUR LIFE. ALTHOUGH, I DO ENJOY WATCHING YOU SPEED RACE TO LIVER FAILURE. CHEERS!

And just like that, our two intrepid, rope-bound heroes were coasting through the stratosphere on a catastrophically high BAC. Naturally, Grand-daddy had conveniently skipped this in his artisanal brew: it wasn't just fermented. It was laced with whatever passes for goblin roofies in this world.

Within seconds, their UI faded to black as both Duncan and Becky rapidly fell over in unison as they reached a deep, chemically enhanced, alcohol-induced coma.

STATUS UPDATE: PASSED OUT

They woke up the next morning entirely devoid of ropes, completely devoid of clothing, and crushed under what could only be described as a mammoth-hide blanket. They were both still riding the dizzying, nauseating waves of the Imp Kombucha.

QUEST UPDATE: KEEP DUNCAN SOBER(ISH)

• The good news: They had somehow managed to save Meowth Vader.
• The bad news: Their short-term memory had been entirely scrubbed from existence, leaving them with absolutely no idea what had happened.

Duncan groaned, sitting up and surveying the immediate area. The entire camp was a chaotic, beautiful disaster—painted with the slaughtered, scattered remains of their goblin captors. It looked as though the entire mob had been utterly eradicated by an unholy trinity of fire magic, clumsy sword wounds, and savage animal claws. Grand-Daddy-Flashes-A-Lot looked particularly worse for wear; all that remained of the perverted imp was a mauled, torn-up corpse and his signature limp member hanging sadly in the dirt.

Becky popped up next. The mammoth blanket immediately betrayed her, sliding precariously down her chest before she frantically scrambled to catch it.

"What the fuck," she whispered, her eyes darting around the carnage. Then she caught a horrifying glimpse of Duncan's shirtless, hairy chest and unapologetic rolls. "No. Fucking. Way. I didn't."

"Oh god, did we?" Duncan repeated, his face draining of whatever color the hangover hadn't already stolen.

They simultaneously recoiled, aggressively clinging to their respective halves of the mammoth hide to salvage whatever microscopic shreds of dignity they had left.

That was when Duncan spotted it.

Sitting casually at the foot of their makeshift bed was an utterly bizarre creature. It possessed jet-black fur, twitching feline ears, and glowing yellow eyes, but it was distinctly humanoid in its bipedalism and leg shape. Its feet were large, padded cat paws housing gigantic, retractable claws, and its hands were a lethal hybrid of human fingers and feline talons.

SYSTEM AI: HOLY SHIT DUNCAN, GIVEN YOUR TRACK RECORD WITH DECISION-MAKING, WAKING UP NAKED NEXT TO AN ONLY FANS STAR AND A GIANT ANTHROPOMORPHIC CAT IS THE MOST LOGICAL PROGRESSION OF YOUR CHARACTER ARC.

The creature sat up slowly, locking its glowing yellow eyes on the panicked warrior.

"You're up," it purred, its voice strangely melodic. "No worries. You and Miss Becky did nothing sexual."

"What the—" Duncan started, but the creature immediately cut him off.

"Oh, yes, this must be quite strange," the humanoid feline continued. "After you saved me, I decided to return the favor and save you. The System gave me a choice: go back home, or join your party. Naturally, I joined. I became a Beastkin, half-cat, half-human."

The tall, clawed entity casually sauntered up to Duncan and affectionately rubbed its furry head against his shoulder.

"It's me, Meowth Vader."
