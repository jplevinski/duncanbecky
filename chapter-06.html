Chapter 6: Earn Your Drink

Part 1

Becky unfroze from the debuff and carefully "pet" the phone on the counter like it was a small dog that just snipped at her.

"Not cool, phone," she scolded. She looked up at Vern and blinked with the practiced seductive head tilt she'd practiced a thousand times.

"Why didn't you want my boobs?" her voice was small, fishing for validation.

"Oh, sweetie," Vern said calmly. "I'm an NPC. A computer-generated character. I don't have a real dick or real desires."

He slid the phone back toward her. Becky tucked it into her sweater pocket and clutched it for a moment. Her shoulders loosened slightly—like the device itself was an emotional support object.

Vern nodded toward the breathalyzer in Duncan's hand. "Now, you'll breathe into that every time it chimes. About once every half hour."

Duncan nodded, gave the device a little shake, and shoved it into his pants pocket.

"And those goblins," Vern continued, "they'll show up on your mini-map. Use the QuestMap app—it'll guide you straight to them."

Duncan pulled out his phone and opened QuestMap. A small arrow marked his position and facing. A dot showed Becky nearby. A red icon pulsed farther out—hostile.

He poked around the interface, toggling quests on and off.

"Aw, hell yes," Duncan grinned. "This is awesome. It's like Questie."

He did a small, dorky, victory jig. "Let's fucking go!"

"Hey, asshole." Becky jabbed him in the shoulder. "What am I supposed to do out there?"

They stepped out of the tavern and into Greater Mellonwood, following the red markers on the map like it was a demonic GPS. As they walked, Duncan launched into an eager crash course—mana vs. health pools, cooldowns, aggro management, positioning, threat tables.

Becky nodded along, occasionally asking a question. She wouldn't remember most of it—but for the first time, Duncan got the sense she was actually listening.

The two approached the marked area on the QuestMap app when a high-pitched, gravelly voice shrieked from the brush.

"Hey, dick-wad! I got somethin' for ya!"

The creature burst into view.

Goblin Porn Star - Level 3

It was about four feet tall. Naked. Green. And gripping an obscenely oversized purple-green dick, which it swung around like a medieval flail. The damn thing had to be a foot long and at the end it was hooked upward like a natural skinned bratwurst.

"Ugh." Becky had seen her fair share of appendages in her adult life. This one was one of the more unpleasant looking. It reminded her of an elderly man she'd... well, she didn't want to think about that. A wave of exhaustion hit her: the desperate wish to be known for literally anything else.

She laid a gentle hand on Duncan's shoulder and leaned in.

"I'm going to tell you something, okay?"

Duncan tilted his head toward her.

She opened her mouth, ready to reveal why she was drowning in Malibu—

CRACK.

A rock slammed into Duncan's skull.

"CHARGE!" Duncan roared, activating the skill as he slammed the goblin with the full force of his 280-pound frame, smashing it flat and driving its own ridiculous member back into its scrotum with a wet thwack.

"Ohhh, my balls, motherfucker!" the goblin screeched as it staggered back, then whipped Duncan across the face with its… appendage. "Cheap shot!"

Duncan's Rage Bar filled fast—70%.

Snarling, he raised his shield and brought it down with surgical cruelty.

Right into the goblin's genitals.

They didn't just break.

Goblin paste, testicle juice, and assorted viscera detonated outward in a horrifying pink mist—most of it splattering directly across Becky's shirt in a thick, slimy streak.

"Why is everything in this world so fucking perverted?" Duncan groaned, turning toward her.

Becky had collapsed to her knees, shoulders shaking, head hanging low.

"You didn't let me finish talking," she sobbed. "Now I'm covered in splooge." She sniffed. "Why does everything want to splooge on me? Is that all I'm good for?"

Duncan stared at her.

"…Jesus, Becky." He sighed. "Enough with the pity party. We still need the ears."

Duncan approached the goblin's body.

Up close, it didn't look fearsome anymore. Its head resembled a tiny, elderly green man—droopy face slack, eyes rolled back, mouth hanging open in quiet defeat. A ridiculous little yellow hat sat crooked atop its purple hair, as if someone had tried to give it dignity at the last second.

The ears were still intact.

Each one was pierced with three metal hoops, no discernible lobe—just cartilage and questionable life choices.

Duncan sighed.

He drew his sword and, with almost surgical disinterest, sliced through the base of each ear and tore them free.

OH SHIT, I ALMOST FORGOT.

ITEM ACQUIRED: FANNY PACK OF CHILDHOOD MEMORIES
Description: It holds shit.
Theme: Princess.
Capacity: 60 items.
Note: You both get one.

Duncan blinked as the fanny pack materialized around his waist.

Pink. Purple. Glittery. Aggressively sparkly.

"…Of course it is."

He stuffed the goblin ears into the shimmering pouch and zipped it shut, the plastic teeth clicking with unsettling cheer.

Part 2

"Hey, a little help next time, splooge-girl!" Duncan barked, wiping pink slime from his chin as he scanned for the next red dot on his mini-map.

Becky's vision blurred. The insult was screaming in her head, louder than the goblin's shrieks.

"Excuse me, what did you just say?" She grabbed her staff, the wood slick with goblin mess, and pointed it directly at Duncan. "SMITE!"

The spell's golden glow ignited, but the letters in her mind did a frantic, shuffle. Instead of lancing toward Duncan, the holy energy spiraled upward and detonated directly into her shoulder.

FRIENDLY FIRE DISABLED (INTERNAL ERROR: USER CONFUSION)

"What the fucking, fucking hell?!" Becky shrieked. Her health bar plummeted by 25% as the radiant backwash scorched her.

Two more goblins burst from the brush, their gravelly laughter sounding like grinding rocks. They didn't use their "weapons" yet; they opted for projectiles.

CRACK.

The first rock caught Becky square in her left eye. The skin split, a splash of hot blood masking her vision. The second rock thudded against her chest, bouncing harmlessly off her chest, but the damage was done—her HP was flashing a rhythmic, panicked red.

"Heal yourself, you dumbass!" Duncan yelled, his self-proclaimed brilliance completely failing to register her emotional collapse.

Embarrassment roared hot at the insult. "Stop fucking calling me stupid!" She whipped her staff in a blind arc, catching Duncan's ankles and sending the chunky warrior face-planting into the dirt.

The goblins took this as an invitation. They both screamed in unison, "Bukkake!" They scurried over Duncan's prone form and began a rhythmic, humiliating assault, beating him with their oversized... things. The damage was a joke—a series of -1 HP chips—but the temporary Stun debuff from the sheer mortification was very real.

Duncan's Rage Bar didn't just fill.

It detonated.

"GET. OFF. ME." He scrambled to his feet, a feral snarl ripping from his throat. He unsheathed his sword and, in one blinding, 100-Rage-fueled slash, cleanly harvested the monsters' members.

A geyser of green and red spray coated Duncan's entire front.

DEBUFF APPLIED: EDGING GONE WRONG
Description: Your movement speed and accuracy have been reduced by 70% due to excessive... viscosity.

Duncan stood frozen, his sword arm heavy with slime, his mini-map obscured by a digital smear of gore. He looked at Becky, who was clutching her eye, and then down at his shimmering Fanny Pack of Childhood Memories now coated in goblin fluids.

"I hate this game," Duncan whispered, tossing Becky her staff as he raised his shield just in time to catch the first goblin's charge.

Becky let out a low, animal growl—for reasons neither her nor Duncan understood—and smote those two motherfuckers to death.

The two goblins died screaming.

The duo collected the ears in silence, then collapsed backward into the grass.

Part 3

"Thank the gods we're done killing those things," Duncan muttered, wiping goblin gore off his shield. "Who the hell dreams this shit up?"

Becky shrugged. "I've heard worse."

He snorted. "Yeah. Comes with being—"

"Duncan, stop." Becky snapped, spinning on him. "I didn't choose it. I failed out of school. Words don't… work right for me."

That stopped him cold.

Duncan opened his mouth, then closed it. He'd dropped out too—but books had always made sense to him. Systems. Rules. Patterns. He'd never had to fight language itself.

Becky stared at the grass. "This place is pulling shit out of my head. Twisting it. Turning it into monsters." She swallowed. "It's doing it to you too—with all this RPG garbage."

"Oh fuck me." Duncan stared down at his sword. "I think you're right. But why?"

Becky shrugged. "No clue. I woke up here after pounding a fifth of Absolut."

The breathalyzer chirped—a crunchy, angry alarm. Duncan fished it out of his pocket and sighed. "Guess it's time."

He inhaled and blew until the device answered with two clean, melodic beeps.

0.16.

His BAC was dropping fast. Way faster than it ever did back home.

Duncan pulled out his phone and opened the quest tracker. Meowth Vader's photo filled the screen with the timer at 23:14:33 until the quest was over.

"I miss him," he said.

Becky leaned closer. "He's cute."

"My mom got him for me when my ex left," Duncan said, a small, tired smile tugging at his mouth. "He's gotten me through a lot."

OK, EMO-KIDS. TIME TO GET A MOVE ON.

"Fuck you AI." Duncan muttered, pushing himself to his feet. "Let's get going, get you cleaned up, and get lit."
