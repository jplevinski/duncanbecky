Chapter 2: First Interactions

Part 1: Duncan

"I want my Munchkin kitty!"

Duncan spun in a slow, unsteady circle, scanning the endless field. No Meowth Vader. No angry chirp. No judgmental goblin face staring up at him.

He sniffed.

"Oh no."

Duncan slowly accepted a terrible truth: he was not seeing his cat again anytime soon.

A notification slammed into his vision.

QUEST ACCEPTED: FIND THE TAVERN OWNER

"What—hey, no, that's not Vader!"

He squinted at the quest text. Read it again.

A tavern.

Duncan paused.

"…Wait a minute."

His face lit up. "A tavern? Aw, yeah. That's what I'm talking about."

He skimmed the details. Village of Mellonwood. Locate the tavern owner, Vern.

"I'm gonna get lit."

YOU ARE ALREADY LIT.

"Why are you like this?" Duncan snapped. "And who even are you? Stop being a buzzkill."

He spotted a pumpkin sitting innocently in the grass. It reminded him—tragically—of Meowth Vader. Duncan's lip trembled.

"I miss you, buddy."

He walked over, smashed the pumpkin with his foot, and sang a slurred song from 1979. Or maybe 1983. Possibly a commercial jingle. Time was a suggestion.

Duncan wandered onward.

The fields were pristine. Unrealistically so. Dandelions dotted the grass—some yellow, some white. He blew the white ones into the air and skipped forward with reckless joy. His chub bounced. His arms flailed. His smile stretched ear to ear.

Duncan was very drunk.

Which was unfortunate, because his life was about to change.

ENEMY DETECTED: SIMP IMP — HE LOVES YOUR GIRL SO MUCH, HEHEHE ;)

"Simp… Imp?" Duncan squinted. He planted his feet in what he hoped was a warrior stance. "Hey, AI, I don't have any weapons."

OH SHIT. SORRY. HERE YOU GO, BUDDY.

A shield materialized in his left hand. A stainless-steel sword appeared in his right.

ITEMS ACQUIRED:
Simple Shield
Stainless Steel Sword

"Oh hell yeah," Duncan grinned. "Thanks, bro."

The Simp Imp charged, shrieking obscenities as it ran.

"Hey fucker! Your girl's so hot. I love her ass. I'd lick it raw all day long!"

Duncan paused.

He considered this.

"I thought I was perverted," he muttered. "But damn. I'm basically a saint."

He waddled forward, lifted his sword, and brought it down hard.

The blade carved across the Imp's bird-nosed, nasty-ass face.

Blood exploded everywhere. Way too much blood. Like a cursed fire hydrant.

The Simp Imp collapsed.

Dead.

+50 XP
LEVEL UP!
Strength +1
Agility +1
Stamina +1

Duncan stared at the notifications.

Then he danced.

ARE YOU TWERKING, BRO?

"Shut up," Duncan said. "I'm happy. I leveled up already!"

He then actually started twerking. His booty moved with purpose.

SERIOUSLY, BRO?

"Yeah," Duncan snapped. "I like moving my butt."

He stopped dancing and looked down.

The corpse was glowing.

Sparkling.

"Oooooh, shit." Duncan crouched. "I think I got loot."

He raised both middle fingers to the sky. "Hey, asshole AI. How do I loot?"

FOCUS ON THE ITEM AND WILL IT INTO YOUR INVENTORY.

Duncan stared at the corpse.

He thought.

He thought harder than he ever had in his life. Harder than the GED exam he failed. Harder than his parents' lectures, which he ignored. He strained every brain cell to its absolute limit.

YOU ARE AN IDIOT, AREN'T YOU?

"Piss off!" Duncan shouted. "I am not an idiot."

He puffed up his chest. "I am, in fact, a dungeon master."

OH REALLY?

"Yeah! I have super creative ideas, fuckass."

OH REALLY?

"My IQ is one-fifty!"

OH REALLY?

"…Fuck you."

OK, GENIUS. WHAT'S OUR NEXT MOVE?

"We get to Vern," Duncan said confidently. "And we get fucked up."

I MUST SAY. BRILLIANT MOVE.

"I know," Duncan replied. "He's got the alcohol I need. Let's fucking go."

Part 2: Becky

"I need another bump. Now."

THIS IS THE LAST ONE.

"Whatever."

Becky snorted the line and threw her head back. "Wooo!"—the kind of sound people made in bad comedy movies right before ruining their lives.

She checked herself instinctively.

Black leggings. White sweater. Booty absolutely doing its job. Hair curled just right. White shoes still pristine, somehow. Becky reached into her sweater pocket.

"…Where's my phone?"

Her stomach dropped.

QUEST ACCEPTED: FIND THE TAVERN OWNER

"Seriously," she snapped. "What happened to my phone?"

YOU LOST IT IN THE WOODS LAST NIGHT. REMEMBER?

Becky groaned and dropped her arms.

OH WAIT- NO YOU DON'T. YOU WERE ABSOLUTELY SMASHED.

"Cool. Great. Fantastic." Becky rubbed her temples. "So no phone. Now what?"

YOU HEAL THAT WARRIOR OVER THERE.

"The ugly one?" Becky glanced sideways.

As if on cue, three Simp Imps popped into existence around Duncan. One latched onto his shin and started chewing. Another climbed him like a greasy jungle gym and bit into his face.

Blood sprayed.

Duncan screamed.

HE NEEDS YOU. HE'LL DIE WITHOUT YOUR HELP.

"Not my problem." Becky shrugged.

She looked around.

Field. Sky. Duncan. Monsters.

That was it.

Her expression shifted.

"…Oh."

The realization hit hard. Duncan had the weapon. When the monsters finished eating him, they'd probably move on to her.

Becky swallowed.

She liked it when Brad ate her. Brad knew what a tongue was for. Soft hair. Pretty eyes.

"Fuck Brad."

A staff appeared in her hand.

JUST POINT IT AT HIM AND SAY 'HEAL.' AND DON'T GET CUTE. YOU HAVE LIMITED MANA.

Becky rolled her eyes. "Ugh. Fine."

She lifted the staff, aimed vaguely at Duncan, and shouted, "Heal!"

Light burst from the staff. Duncan's wounds closed in a flash of gold.

He didn't waste the moment.

Duncan roared and went to work—smashing and slashing the Simp Imps until they burst apart into wet, twitching chunks of monster meat.

Silence returned to the field.

Notifications bloomed.

+50 XP
LEVEL UP! +1 Intelligence, +1 Spirit
NEW SPELL UNLOCKED: SMITE

Becky blinked.

"…Wait."

She smiled slowly as she read the spell's description. Cost: 20MP, deals holy damage to an enemy based on your intelligence and spirit.

"Oh."

That changed things.

END OF CHAPTER.

Interlude: The Architect

He threw back another shot. The burn was familiar—comforting.

On the monitor, Duncan twerked over a sparkling corpse, laughing like nothing hurt yet.

"Too easy," he muttered, "needs recalibration".

With a flick of his finger, he nudged some sliders up, absurdity, intensity. Another swallow. The numbers wavered, then settled. He hesitated—just long enough to feel it—before flipping the Trauma Excavation switch on and adjusting it up as well.
