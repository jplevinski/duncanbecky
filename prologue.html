Prologue

This is a story about Duncan.

Duncan owns a Munchkin cat named Meowth Vader. Duncan lives in his mother's basement, a subterranean kingdom of crusty manga stacks, unpainted mini-figurines, and the constant buzz of gaming PC fans. When Duncan drinks, he plucks his nose hairs with obsessive, watery-eyed precision. Duncan drinks a lot.

Duncan is thirty years old.

On Tuesday, Duncan's guild finally downed a legendary raid boss in World of Warcraft Classic. He celebrated the victory by murdering a fifth of Jägermeister and polishing off an entire case of hard seltzers. He eventually blacked out to the flickering glow of big-booty porn and the sweet, dizzying scent of his own success.

He awoke in the middle of a sun-drenched field, staring at a floating user interface and a character selection screen.

***

This is a story about Becky.

Becky has a boyfriend named Brad. Becky drives a blue Mustang and calls herself an influencer, which is a fancy way of saying she lives off the generosity of men who fund her "content." When Becky drinks, she plucks her eyebrows until they are thin, symmetrical lines of regret. Becky drinks a lot.

Becky is twenty-four years old.

On Tuesday, Becky walked in on Brad fingering her best friend, Emily. Becky did not celebrate. She fled into the woods behind the house and surrendered to a bottle of Absolut Vodka. She eventually blacked out in a pile of damp leaves, sobbing herself into a chemical stupor.

She awoke in the middle of a sun-drenched field, staring at a floating user interface and a character selection screen.

This is a story about Duncan and Becky. It is the story of how their worlds collided—and how two people who can't even manage their own blood-alcohol levels now have to rely on each other to survive.
