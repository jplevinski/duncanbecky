Chapter 5: Vern

Part 1

The tavern, surprisingly, was the most normal-looking building in the entire town. Whoever designed it actually seemed to care about the place. The cardboard exterior featured meticulously hand-drawn brickwork, and a set of old-timey saloon doors had been added for a touch of "Drunk-West" charm. It was mercifully devoid of pink or purple—just raw cardboard brown and a deep, stained oak.

"Finally!" Duncan exclaimed.

His Malibu-equipped partner had vanished into the haze, and his BAC was starting to plummet. Something about being such a titan of a man meant he metabolized alcohol like a demon on crack.

"Becky! I found the bar, you ho!" Duncan bellowed.

The townsfolk in the square stopped dead. Their eyes flashed with a faint, synchronized red glow—as if they were all recording the transgression—before the light faded and the crowd returned to their grumpy, cardboard routines.

"Oh... okay," Duncan shivered. "That was weird".

NO WEIRDER THAN AN UNEMPLOYED 30-YEAR-OLD PLAYING WOW IN HIS MOTHER'S BASEMENT.

"I have a job! I'm a professional Virtual Dungeon Master!" Duncan shot back defensively. "And Mom won't buy me booze, anyway. I also donate plasma, asshole!"

YOU PASS THE HEALTH SCREEN? HOW?

"I'm 280 pounds of pure health. Monkey-fuck." Duncan marched up to the saloon doors and shoved them open with the fervor of a man who could feel the sobriety monster clawing at his throat.

"Narrator... that's getting a little too close to home, buddy," Duncan muttered to the empty air.

Meanwhile, the Narrator took another pull of 99 Pineapples, chased it with a swig of Mixed Berry Powerade, and contemplated the series of catastrophic life choices that had led to this moment.

Becky stumbled in right behind him. "Hey, fucker." She had clearly polished off the rest of the Malibu on the short walk from the gate.

The interior was a revelation. It wasn't cardboard. It was nice—disturbingly nice. It was a high-class, all-wood joint straight out of a 1920s speakeasy, smelling of expensive tobacco and cedar.

At the counter stood a man who looked like he'd forgotten how to smile in the previous century.

QUEST COMPLETE: FIND THE TAVERN OWNER
Rewards: Access to Vern's Bar

Part 2

Vern looked at the two lushes with a deep, disappointed stare. "Ain't get too many outsiders in here. But I got something for you."

Vern reached down to the bar and pulled out a bottle of Sambuca and poured two shots. "Now take 'em." He also pulled a tablet out of his apron.

"A tablet, in a old tavern, this makes no sense?" Duncan said.

"Can I check my socials?" Becky begged.

"Come now. Stay with me." Vern scrolled through his tablet and pulled up the App Quest-Now. It displayed the objectives and the quest name. It was a very strange quest name indeed.

QUEST: KEEP DUNCAN SOBER(ISH)
Objective: Maintain blood-alcohol content above point one for the next twenty-four hours.

The quest had something familiar on it that Duncan immediately recognized. "Meowth Vader!"

"Oh, so you know the cat. Seemed strange this one came by." Vern scratched his beard. "It looks like Meowth Vader has been catnapped and to get him back you need to stay drunk."

"That's really fucked up." Becky said as she took her shots.

"It's serious, look at that failure penalty."

FAILURE PENALTY: MEOWTH VADER DELETED. OR EUTHANIZED, WHICHEVER SOUNDS HARSHER TO YOU.

Duncan's face went from vodka-flush to ghost-white.

"No. No no no no. AI you absolute bastard." He looked at Vern. Slammed his fists on the counter, and promptly asked for more Sambuca along with 2 shots of Five O'clock and another V8, just to be sure his BAC was high enough.

"Aye, yes, but also look at the reward." Vern flicked his finger scrolling to the rewards section of the quest.

Rewards: Cat back, 500 XP, one (1) bag of catnip.

"Now drink, hero. You're at 0.07." Vern handed him a breathalyzer to keep track of his BAC.

Becky squinted through her Malibu haze. "Is your cat fat?"

"Shut up. He's plush." Duncan said. "He's my baby."

She shrugged, took another shot. "Well. If the kitty dies we can always get you a new pet. Maybe a Molly or a Gerbil?"

Duncan stared daggers. "If Meowth Vader dies I will end you first, I swear on every raid I ever wiped on."

POOF.

A digital stopwatch appeared—ticking down from 24:00:00.

Duncan grabbed the nearest bottle—something labeled 'Dragon's Piss Vodka'—and tilted it back until the sear hit his throat became too much.

Duncan took a look at the photo of his short-legged black cat in the quest description and muttered, "Daddy's coming, Vader."

Part 3

Vern's stare lingered on the two miscreants, heavy with a century's worth of professional disappointment. "Those drinks ain't free, ya know?"

Duncan blew into the breathalyzer. 0.175.

It was a solid start—comfortably above the 0.100 threshold, yet still under the 0.200 mark where balance becomes a suggestion and aiming becomes a theory. "We have to pay? I don't have any money."

Becky crossed her arms, her face twisting into a pouty sneer. "I don't have money either." For reasons known only to her and the Malibu, she decided this was the moment to peel off her white, bird-shit-stained sweatshirt. She revealed a low-cut black undershirt and leaned over the bar, batting her eyes at Vern. "I'm sure we can work something out, right?"

"Yes'm, we can." Vern didn't even blink. He produced a tablet, tapped an icon for the BarTenderHelper app, and swiveled the screen toward them.

The display showed two tabs. Each had been granted a 20-credit limit; both were currently maxed out by the rounds Vern had already poured.

GUESS YOU BOTH NEED REAL JOBS.

"Fuck you, AI," they roared in unison.

"I got something for ya both." Vern took out two devices that looked suspiciously like high-end smartphones. "These are for young adventurers like yourselves." He swiped through a few menus before tapping an app labeled QuestBoard.

A long list of tasks populated the screen, filterable by location, level, and reward. Vern set the location to "Greater Mellonwood" and the level range to 1–5.

ACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED: REAL WORK
Playtime's over, you two disasters. Go slay something, fetch something, or die trying. Either way, find a way to pay your bar tab.

"Vern, that's not exactly what I had in mind." Becky leaned in even closer, reaching out to rest a hand on his shoulder.

"Ma'am—" Vern began.

Becky's eyes snapped wide. She froze, her gaze darting frantically down her own body—inspecting her waist, her chest, her hands—desperately verifying her shelf life. Once she was certain she hadn't spontaneously aged twenty years, her eyes narrowed into venomous slits.

"Who are you calling ma'am? Do I look like a middle-aged soccer mom to you?"

Duncan let out a laugh so sharp and jagged it sounded like a dingo barking. Before he could catch his breath, Becky's hand whipped away from Vern and cracked squarely across Duncan's mouth.

Duncan's lip split instantly. He wiped a smear of blood away, his eyes narrowing. "Okay, missy," he mocked.

Becky growled in frustration, plopped back onto her barstool, and aggressively yanked her sweatshirt back on. "Just give me the stupid phone!" she snapped, snatching the device from the counter.

Her thumb became a blur. "Where's Insta? Where's Snapchat? Where is literally anything that matters?"

She scrolled frantically, her face twisting as she navigated a UI that looked like something from her childhood. Instead of the familiar flood of likes and comments, she was met with a grid of sterile icons: Stats, QuestLog, Inventory.

"Ugh, fuck this trash!"

She slammed the phone down in a fit.

CRACKLE.

A surge of blue electricity arced from the device, zapping Becky hard. Her body locked up, and a translucent 30-second timer appeared over her head, ticking down the duration of her Stunned debuff.

THESE AREN'T YOUR ORDINARY PHONES, BECKY. THEY FIGHT BACK WHEN ABUSED.

Duncan poked Becky's frozen shoulder and giggled. "You think we can clean her up?"

"Showers will cost you 25 credits." Vern handed Duncan the second phone. "Take this quest right here. It's an easy one. It'll net you 50 credits—enough for a wash, another round of Dragon's Piss and to pay off that tab of yours."

QUEST ACCEPTED: EARN YOUR KEEP
Objective: The Goblins of Greater Mellonwood have invaded the storage pits and are savagely eating all the potatoes.
Goal: Slay 3 Goblins and bring their bloodied ears to Vern the Bartender as proof of their demise.
Reward: 50 Credits, 200 XP, 1 x Mystery Spud Juice.
